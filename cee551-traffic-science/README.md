# CEE 551 · Traffic Science

[All courses / 全部课程](../README.md)

Graduate course at the University of Michigan. Xingmin Wang served as a co-instructor in Fall 2022, 2023, and 2024. These materials are preserved from the [original course page](https://xingminw.github.io/teaching/cee551/).

密歇根大学研究生课程。王兴民于 2022、2023、2024 年秋季联合授课。本目录按主题整理现有 PDF 课件，供学生复习与公开学习。

## Course overview / 课程内容

Traffic flow theory, traffic control, and traffic simulation, including fundamental diagrams, the LWR model, the cell transmission model, signal timing, coordination, and connected and automated transportation. The traffic-flow slides are the 2024 teaching materials. The signal-control module was taught in 2022–2023.
